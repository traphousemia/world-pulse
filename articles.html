World Population News & Recent Events

Fact-checked summaries of current events affecting world population, global health, and demographics

Every day, events around the world shift the global population count — earthquakes, disease outbreaks, conflicts, and public health breakthroughs all leave a mark on the numbers that World Pulse tracks in real time. This page collects recent, sourced news items covering those events: seismic activity with casualty potential, infectious disease outbreaks, mortality trends, and demographic developments from verified primary sources like the USGS, WHO, CDC, and ReliefWeb.

Each summary is drawn directly from official reporting — no speculation, no AI-generated content. Every item links back to the original source so you can read the full report yourself. World Pulse publishes new articles automatically twice daily when significant events are detected, and manually when major humanitarian or demographic news breaks.
