Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how World Pulse ("we," "us," or "this site") handles information when you visit worldpulse.fyi (the "Site"). We built this page to be genuinely useful and to be straightforward about the fact that, like most ad-supported sites, we rely on third-party advertising partners who use cookies.

Information We Collect Directly

World Pulse does not require account creation, does not collect names, email addresses, or payment information, and does not operate a server-side database of visitor activity. The population, birth, and death figures shown are computed entirely in your browser from publicly published statistics — no personal data is involved in generating them.

Cookies and Third-Party Advertising

We use (or intend to use) Google AdSense to display advertisements on this Site. Google and its partners use cookies — including the DoubleClick DART cookie — to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this Site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

For more detail on how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Cookie Consent

On your first visit, this Site displays a cookie notice allowing you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. Your choice is stored locally in your browser. If you are visiting from the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, please note that this banner is a basic notice-and-choice mechanism; full regulatory compliance for EEA/UK traffic requires a certified Consent Management Platform, which the site operator is responsible for integrating prior to serving personalized ads to those regions.

Log Data

Like most websites, the hosting infrastructure serving this Site may automatically log standard technical information such as IP address, browser type, referring page, and timestamps. This data is used only in aggregate for security and performance monitoring, not to identify individual visitors.

Children's Privacy

This Site is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at contact@worldpulse.fyi.
